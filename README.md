### Hi, I'm João Paulo 🌲

Ph.D. candidate in forest sciences at **Université Laval** (Québec). I study **which tree communities and species will thrive where as the climate changes**, using forest inventory plots, airborne and spaceborne **lidar**, soil data and species distribution models, and I try to turn the results into tools that foresters can use.
